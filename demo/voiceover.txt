Army of LoRAs. Type one prompt in ComfyUI, and an army of sixty-five community LoRAs decides how to render it, across five open image and video models.

Hugging Face has thousands of great LoRAs. But which one fits your prompt, on which base model, at what strength, with which trigger word?

So the router decides. The prompt is embedded with pybed, our static int-eight embedding model. There's no transformer, just a token lookup, so it takes about two milliseconds on the CPU. It scores every LoRA's description, picks image or video, and deploys the model whose army fits best.

I scraped seven hundred and fifty-five adapters, dropped anything adult, any likeness or franchise, and anything unlicensed, then hand-picked sixty-five.
